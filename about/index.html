WHO WE ARE

Technology grounded in real business needs.

HICAD Systems Limited is a Nigerian computer and communications consultancy established in 1989. The company has a long-standing focus on providing Information Technology services to the Nigerian insurance industry.

Our experience also includes the implementation of computerised payroll and human resource applications, as well as e-procurement solutions. We bring a practical, delivery-focused approach to the technology needs of organisations.
